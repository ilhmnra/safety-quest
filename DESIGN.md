First-person factory training for desktop and portrait mobile players. The voxel factory supplies the visual identity and spatial context.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1 for the interface. Gameplay cinematics use brief, purposeful motion and honor the reduced-motion setting.

- Use the existing cream text and steel-green surfaces. Reserve amber for the current decision or warning, so it carries meaning.
- Use Segoe UI for instructions and the existing monospace for machine and station identifiers. The latter echoes control labels without slowing down longer copy.
- Anchor the reach question to the inspection view with a compact lower panel and a transparent backdrop. Keep the lodged object in view while the two actions sit together below it.
- Use the established 4 px spacing increments and keep decision buttons at least 44 px high. Keep their labels and visible focus rings intact on narrow screens.
- Give Works 98 one purpose: running the training mail and security workflow. Its compact title bar and taskbar communicate which window is open.
- Give professional content one entry from each game menu. In the directory, put the featured Safety Quest summary and its case-study link together under Projects.
- Default keyboard focus to Cancel for the unsafe reach prompt. Tab stays inside the prompt and Escape dismisses it. Keep outlines visible for both actions.
- Keep the successful ending music independent of directory tab changes. Audio controls govern the shared output level.
